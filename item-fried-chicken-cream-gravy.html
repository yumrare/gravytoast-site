Plates & Classics

Fried Chicken with Cream Gravy

Buttermilk fried chicken served with the cream gravy made from its own drippings. The Sunday-dinner plate of the South.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionThe Deep South
Classic baseThe chicken, with biscuits or potatoes
Prep timeAbout 45 minutes
StorageStore chicken and gravy separately, refrigerated.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; chicken keeps 3 days, gravy likewise
Best usesSunday dinners, supper plates, church suppers
SubstitutesChicken-fried chicken, the boneless form of the same plate
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Fried chicken with cream gravy is the plate the Southern Sunday was built around before air conditioning, when the big cooking happened after church. The gravy is made while the chicken rests — that pause is part of the recipe.

Making it

Fry the chicken in a heavy skillet and hold it warm. Pour off all but a few spoonfuls of drippings, build the cream gravy in the pan, and serve it over and beside the chicken.

Serving it

Chicken on a platter, gravy in a boat or poured at the table. Biscuits travel with it to catch what the chicken misses.

Swaps & pairings

Gravy over the chicken crisps nothing — houses divide between poured and passed. The catalog records both services.
