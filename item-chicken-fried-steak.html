Chicken-Fried Steak with Cream Gravy

A tenderized round steak, battered and fried like chicken, drowned in country cream or pepper gravy. The Texas plate that defines cream gravy's purpose.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionTexas
Classic baseThe steak itself, with mashed potatoes
Prep timeAbout 35 minutes
StorageComponents separately, briefly; the plate is made to order.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; steak reheats poorly
Best usesSupper plates, steakhouse menus, Texas cafés
SubstitutesChicken-fried chicken for the same method on poultry
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chicken-fried steak is a Texas-German inheritance — schnitzel technique in beef country — and the gravy is not a garnish but half the dish. A dry CFS is a failed plate; the standard is gravy to the plate's edge.

Making it

Pound cube steak thin, dredge in seasoned flour, dip in egg wash, dredge again, and fry crisp. Make the cream gravy in the same pan from the drippings and pour it over at the table.

Serving it

On a warm plate, gravy over the steak and the mashed potatoes in one pour. Knife work is expected; the crust softens as it sits.

Swaps & pairings

Pepper gravy makes it the steakhouse version; plain cream gravy the café version. Some houses serve the gravy on the side to protect the crust — the catalog notes it without endorsing it.
