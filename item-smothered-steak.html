Smothered Steak

Round steak braised tender in brown onion gravy. The diner and farmhouse method for turning a tough cut into a supper plate.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseMashed potatoes or egg noodles
Prep time1 to 2 hours, mostly braising
StorageStore in its gravy, refrigerated.
Shelf lifeKeeps 3 to 4 days and improves; freezes well
Best usesSupper plates, Sunday dinners, diner specials
SubstitutesSwiss steak, the tomato-braised variant
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Smothered steak turns the cheapest cut in the case into the plate everyone asks about, which was always the point. Round steak, time, and onion gravy: the diner belt's alchemy.

Making it

Flour and brown round steak pieces; build an onion gravy in the pan; return the steak and braise low one to two hours until it cuts with a fork. The gravy thickens as it cooks.

Serving it

Over mashed potatoes or noodles, the gravy ladled over everything. A Sunday plate made from a weekday cut.

Swaps & pairings

Tomatoes in the braise make Swiss steak. Mushrooms added to the gravy borrow the mushroom entry's depth.
