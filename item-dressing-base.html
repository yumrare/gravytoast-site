Leftover Dressing Base

Leftover baked dressing or stuffing, reheated crisp, used as a base for turkey and giblet gravy. The day-after plate that justifies making extra.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionUnited States holiday kitchens
Classic baseTurkey gravy, giblet gravy
Prep timeAbout 20 minutes to reheat crisp
StorageRefrigerate promptly after the holiday meal; freeze portions for later plates.
Shelf life3 to 4 days refrigerated, within the leftovers' life
Best usesLeftover plates, hot turkey sandwiches, breakfast patties
SubstitutesA cornbread square; a slice of bread pudding's savory kin
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The dressing base is holiday economy at its best: the bread-and-cornbread crumb that was baked to hold turkey gravy becomes, a day later, the thing the gravy is poured on. Nothing wasted, twice served.

Making it

Press leftover dressing into a pan, chill if loose, and reheat in a hot oven or slice and fry until the edges crisp. The texture should go from scoop to slab.

Serving it

As the platform of the leftover plate: dressing, sliced turkey, gravy over all. Also fried in patties under a fried egg and gravy.

Swaps & pairings

Fried as patties it becomes a breakfast base under eggs. The cornbread-dressing South and bread-stuffing North both claim the method.
