About it

McCormick & Company was founded in Baltimore in 1889 and became the dominant American spice house. Its gravy line — brown, turkey, chicken, country — is the supermarket shelf's reference set, sold in packets and jars.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its brown gravy mix is the packet form of this catalog's brown gravy, and its turkey gravy jar is the holiday shortcut against the scratch turkey entry.

Serving it

National supermarket shelves; the brand's gravies appear at holiday tables as the second gravy beside the scratch one.

Swaps & pairings

The catalog lists it for its Delmarva ground as much as its shelf share: Maryland is diner country, and its counters run on packet gravy.
