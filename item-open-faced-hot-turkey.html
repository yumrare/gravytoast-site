Open-Faced Hot Turkey

Sliced roast turkey on bread, open-faced, covered in turkey gravy with mashed potatoes alongside. The diner special built from the holiday bird.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseWhite toast or a split-top roll
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes with cooked turkey
StorageStore turkey and gravy separately, up to 3 to 4 days.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once assembly of leftovers
Best usesDiner specials, leftover plates, supper
SubstitutesGiblet gravy for the old-style plate
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The hot turkey plate is the diner's evergreen special — Thanksgiving rehearsed weekly. Its open face is structural: the top slice would hide the gravy, and hiding the gravy is not done.

Making it

Lay sliced roast turkey over toast or a griddled split-top roll, ladle hot turkey gravy across the whole plate, and crown the mashed potatoes with a second ladle.

Serving it

Open-faced, always; cranberry on the side in diners that honor the original. The day-after-Thanksgiving version uses the dressing base.

Swaps & pairings

On the dressing base it becomes the leftover plate entire. With chicken and chicken gravy it is the hot chicken sandwich of the same counters.
