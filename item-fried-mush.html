Toasts & Bases

Fried Mush

Cooked cornmeal chilled firm, sliced, and fried crisp. The Midwest and Appalachian leftover base that takes gravy or syrup with equal grace.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionMidwest and Appalachia
Classic baseCornmeal gravy, sausage gravy
Prep timeChill overnight; 10 minutes to fry
StorageKeep the molded loaf wrapped in the refrigerator until slicing.
Shelf lifeThe chilled loaf keeps 5 days refrigerated
Best usesBreakfast bases, gravy plates, syrup plates
SubstitutesFried grits cakes; fried polenta slices
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Fried mush was the farmhouse answer to leftover cornmeal porridge, chilled in a loaf pan overnight and fried in the morning. Maple syrup is its sweet partner; gravy is its savory one.

Making it

Pour hot cooked cornmeal into a loaf pan and chill until firm. Slice half an inch thick, flour lightly if liked, and fry in bacon fat or butter until both sides are deeply golden.

Serving it

Under cornmeal or sausage gravy at breakfast, or with syrup as its own plate. Two slices per serving is the farmhouse measure.

Swaps & pairings

Fry it in bacon fat for gravy service, in butter for syrup. A cornmeal-dredged slice crisps harder and stands up to a full ladle.
