The Ozarks

The Missouri and Arkansas Ozarks are chocolate-gravy country, where the sweet cocoa gravy over biscuits is a family tradition with roots cooks still debate.

Chocolate Gravy

A sweet breakfast gravy of cocoa, sugar, flour, and milk, cooked like a thin pudding and ladled over hot biscuits. An Ozark and Appalachian tradition, served as a treat, not an everyday gravy.

Biscuits with Chocolate Gravy

Hot buttermilk biscuits split and covered in chocolate gravy, the sweet breakfast plate of the Ozarks and Appalachia. A treat plate, made for weekends and grandchildren.
