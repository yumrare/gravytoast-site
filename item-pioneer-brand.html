About it

Pioneer Brand has milled in San Antonio since 1851. Its gravy mixes — country, peppered, sausage-flavored — were built for the Texas café trade and became the packet standard in Southern pantries.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: the brand's peppered gravy mix — just add milk — is the diner-shortcut version of this catalog's pepper gravy entry, seasoned to be finished with cracked pepper.

Serving it

Its mixes stand behind café counters and home biscuit plates across Texas and the South, wherever a scratch pan is not practical.

Swaps & pairings

The catalog profiles mix makers neutrally alongside scratch gravies; the Gravy Chart's entries describe the scratch forms these mixes approximate.
