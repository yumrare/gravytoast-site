The Deep South

The lowland South runs on sausage gravy, red-eye, and tomato gravy. Its farmhouse breakfasts built the canon the rest of the country now eats in diners.

Sausage Gravy

The national diner standard: crumbled pork breakfast sausage folded through a peppered milk gravy, served over biscuits. If a menu says biscuits and gravy, this is the gravy.

Gravies · The Deep South

Red-Eye Gravy

A thin, sharp gravy of country-ham drippings and black coffee, named for the circle of red fat that floats on it like an eye. It is sopped, not poured, over ham and biscuits.

Gravies · The Deep South

Tomato Gravy

A roux-based gravy built on tomatoes, canned or fresh, with bacon fat or drippings. Tangy and brick-red, it is poured over biscuits, rice, or grits across the South.

Gravies · The Deep South

Ham Gravy

A thickened milk gravy built on baked-ham drippings and pan juices, the gravy of the holiday ham table. Creamier and milder than red-eye gravy, its coffee-sharp cousin.

Gravies · The Deep South

Chicken Gravy

A pale, rich gravy from roast or fried chicken drippings and stock, flour-thickened. The gravy of Sunday chicken dinners, mashed potatoes, and chicken-fried chicken.

Gravies · The Deep South

Milk Gravy

The plain form of the Southern white gravy: fat, flour, and milk, whisked smooth and peppered. Every sausage, sawmill, and country gravy starts from this pan.

Gravies · The Deep South

Buttermilk Biscuit

The Southern buttermilk biscuit: tall, tender, and tangy, built to be split and gravied. The single most important base in this catalog.

Toasts & Bases · The Deep South

Cornbread

A wedge or square of skillet cornbread, split or crumbled as the base. In Louisiana it carries crawfish gravy; in the upland South, cornmeal and tomato gravies.

Toasts & Bases · The Deep South

Sausage Gravy over Biscuits

The signature plate of the gravy world: split buttermilk biscuits under a full ladle of sausage gravy. The dish biscuits and gravy means, everywhere it is served.

Plates & Classics · The Deep South

Country Ham with Red-Eye Gravy

A thick slice of fried country ham with its red-eye gravy, biscuits on the side for sopping. The upland South's original gravy plate.

Plates & Classics · The Deep South

Fried Chicken with Cream Gravy

Buttermilk fried chicken served with the cream gravy made from its own drippings. The Sunday-dinner plate of the South.

Plates & Classics · The Deep South

Smothered Pork Chops

Pork chops browned, then braised in their own onion gravy until tender. The smothered plate: meat and gravy cooked into one thing.
