Turkey Gravy

Roast-turkey drippings and stock, flour-thickened and strained smooth. Giblet gravy's plainer sibling: the same holiday bird, without the chopped giblets.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionNew England
Classic baseRoast turkey and mashed potatoes
Prep timeAbout 20 minutes, built during roasting
StorageRefrigerate sealed; chill fast after the meal, it is a rich stock.
Shelf life3 to 4 days refrigerated; freezes well
Best usesRoast turkey, dressing, mashed potatoes, leftover sandwiches
SubstitutesChicken stock and drippings for a smaller bird
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Turkey gravy is giblet gravy's plainer sibling and the one most tables now serve. Its craft is in the stock: a gravy is only as good as the bird, so the neck and wings simmer while the turkey roasts.

Making it

Make a roux from skimmed roasting drippings, add turkey stock and defatted pan juices, and simmer smooth. Strain for a silky finish. A splash of cream or a knob of butter at the end is a modern touch.

Serving it

In the gravy boat at the holiday table, and the next day over open-faced hot turkey sandwiches — its second career.

Swaps & pairings

Fold in chopped giblets and it becomes giblet gravy. A spoon of soy or a dash of sherry deepens a pale batch.
