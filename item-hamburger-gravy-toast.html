Hamburger Gravy over Toast

Ground-beef gravy ladled over toast points: the cafeteria and farmhouse descendant of SOS, and a main-dish plate in its own right.

About it

Hamburger gravy over toast is SOS rebuilt with fresh ground beef — the version school cafeterias and farmhouse kitchens actually raised generations on. It teaches the same lesson as its parent: gravy makes a little meat feed many.

Making it

Brown ground beef with onion, build the peppery milk gravy in the same pan, and ladle over toast points — two slices, cut corner to corner, per plate.

Serving it

Over toast at supper, with peas or potatoes alongside. In school lunchrooms it arrived on mashed potatoes instead — same gravy, different base.

Swaps & pairings

Over egg noodles it becomes a stroganoff-adjacent supper. A dash of Worcestershire is the cafeteria signature.
