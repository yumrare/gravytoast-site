Liver and Onion Gravy Plate

Floured liver slices fried and returned to their pan gravy with caramelized onions. The diner plate that regulars order by heart.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseMashed potatoes alongside

StorageCook to order; leftovers lose the plate's point.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; liver waits for no one
Best usesDiner plates, supper, farmhouse cooking
SubstitutesOnion gravy over Salisbury steak for the liver-shy
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Liver and onions is the plate diners keep on the menu for the regulars who would riot without it. The gravy is what separates a good plate from a dry one, and the onions must be sweet enough to argue with the liver.

Making it

Soak liver in milk if liked, flour it, and fry briefly — overcooked liver is the plate's only true failure. Build the gravy in the pan, soften the onions in it, and reunite everything for a minute.

Serving it

Liver slices shingled on the plate, onions and gravy over all, mashed potatoes taking the overflow.

Swaps & pairings

Calf liver is the tender classic, beef liver the diner economy cut. Bacon fried first and crumbled over is the belt's garnish.
