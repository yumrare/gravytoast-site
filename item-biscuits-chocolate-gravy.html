Biscuits with Chocolate Gravy

Hot buttermilk biscuits split and covered in chocolate gravy, the sweet breakfast plate of the Ozarks and Appalachia. A treat plate, made for weekends and grandchildren.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionThe Ozarks

Prep timeAbout 25 minutes
StorageStore gravy sealed with wrap on its surface.

Best usesBreakfast treats, holiday mornings
SubstitutesCornbread under chocolate gravy, the dessert reading
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chocolate gravy biscuits are the plate Ozark and Appalachian families describe with the word 'Grandma.' It reads as dessert and eats as breakfast, and children raised on it defend the classification fiercely.

Making it

Bake biscuits; make the chocolate gravy like a thin pudding while they bake. Split the biscuits, butter them lightly, and ladle the gravy over while both are hot.

Serving it

Two biscuits per plate under a full ladle, bacon alongside for the salt. Served at breakfast without apology.

Swaps & pairings

Dark cocoa and less sugar makes the adult plate. A crumble of bacon on top is the modern diner flourish.
