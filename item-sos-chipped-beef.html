Creamed Chipped Beef on Toast (SOS)

Creamed chipped beef on toast: dried beef folded into a white sauce over toast points. The military plate — SOS — that came home from the mess hall to the diner.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionThe Military Tradition
Classic baseWhite toast
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes
StorageRefrigerate the creamed beef sealed; toast is made to order.
Shelf lifeThe creamed beef keeps 3 days refrigerated; toast fresh
Best usesBreakfast plates, diner specials, mess-hall tradition
SubstitutesHamburger gravy is the ground-beef descendant
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

SOS began as a way to stretch preserved beef across a garrison and became the dish veterans asked for at home. Its initials are famously unprintable in full; mess halls softened them to 'same old stuff.' The dish outlived the joke.

Making it

Rinse and shred dried chipped beef; simmer it briefly in a plain milk gravy — butter, flour, milk — without salt until tasted. Pour over toast points, two slices per plate.

Serving it

Over toast on a mess tray or diner plate, gravy soaking to the edges. Delaware diners keep it famous; veterans keep it ordered.

Swaps & pairings

On an English muffin it turns Northern-diner. With the beef browned in butter first, it gains the color some cooks insist on.
