Tomato Gravy over Grits

A bowl of stone-ground grits under brick-red tomato gravy, the Lowcountry supper plate. Summer's answer to shrimp and grits.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionThe Lowcountry
Classic baseGrits
Prep timeAbout 45 minutes with stone-ground grits
StorageStore grits and gravy separately, refrigerated.
Shelf lifeComponents keep 3 to 4 days; assemble hot
Best usesSupper plates, summer cooking, breakfast bowls
SubstitutesOver rice for the inland version; over biscuits at breakfast
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Tomato gravy over grits is the Lowcountry's supper answer to shrimp and grits' breakfast fame — the same bowl, the garden standing in for the creek. In tomato season it is made weekly in coastal kitchens.

Making it

Cook grits low and loose; make the tomato gravy on a bacon-fat roux with fresh or canned tomatoes. Pool the gravy over the grits and finish with green onion or parsley.

Serving it

In a wide bowl, grits first, gravy pooled on top. Fried pork chops or fish alongside make it a full supper.

Swaps & pairings

Cream stirred into the gravy makes the pink Lowcountry variant. A poached egg on top carries it to brunch.
