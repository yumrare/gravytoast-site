Vegetarian Mushroom Gravy

A meatless mushroom gravy built on vegetable stock and mushroom liquor, roux- or cornstarch-thickened. The holiday table's vegetarian anchor, made to stand beside the turkey gravy.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionUnited States

About it

The vegetarian mushroom gravy was standardized by holiday necessity: a second gravy for the vegetarian at the turkey table. Built right, with hard-browned mushrooms and real stock, it is nobody's compromise.

Making it

Brown mushrooms hard in butter or oil until their liquor cooks away. Add flour or a cornstarch slurry with vegetable stock, soy sauce, and thyme. Simmer until dark and savory.

Serving it

In its own boat at the holiday table, over mashed potatoes, stuffing, and vegetarian roasts. It also anchors a meatless biscuits-and-gravy.

Swaps & pairings

Dried porcini and their soaking liquid make it taste like a forest floor. Miso or nutritional yeast deepen it without meat.
