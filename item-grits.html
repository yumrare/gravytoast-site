Toasts & Bases

Grits

Ground hominy corn cooked to a soft porridge, the Lowcountry's base. Under shrimp gravy it is the region's defining plate; under tomato gravy, its supper form.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionThe Lowcountry
Classic baseShrimp gravy, tomato gravy
Prep time5 to 45 minutes by grade
StorageRefrigerate cooked grits in a loaf pan — set grits slice and fry as a second base.
Shelf lifeCooked grits keep 4 to 5 days refrigerated and set solid
Best usesShrimp and grits, breakfast bowls, supper plates
SubstitutesPolenta, the Italian cousin; mashed potatoes off-region
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Grits are milled from nixtamal-treated corn — hominy — which is why they taste different from cornmeal mush. The stone-ground grade must be cooked low and long; quick grits are the diner's compromise.

Making it

Simmer stone-ground grits in salted water 40 minutes or more, stirring, until creamy; finish with butter. Quick grits take 5 minutes and less character. Keep them loose; they set as they stand.

Serving it

In a bowl with the gravy pooled on top and the shrimp or ham at the center. At breakfast they sit beside eggs; at supper they carry the plate.

Swaps & pairings

Set and fried, grits become a crisp base of their own. Cheese grits move the plate toward brunch; plain grits keep the gravy in charge.
