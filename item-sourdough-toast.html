Toasts & Bases

Sourdough Toast

Tangy, open-crumb toast from a levain loaf. Its acidity cuts rich gravies, and its crust stands up to a full ladle better than soft breads.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionUnited States, from European levain baking
Classic baseTomato gravy, chili gravy, cheese gravies
Prep time3 to 4 minutes to toast
StorageStore cut-side down on a board or in a cloth bag; never refrigerate bread.
Shelf lifeThe loaf keeps 4 to 5 days; day-old sourdough toasts best
Best usesGravy plates, breakfast, open-faced plates
SubstitutesA rustic country loaf; rye for a different tang
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Sourdough's acidity is the asset: tomato and chili gravies that flatten white toast come alive on it. West Coast diners made it a breakfast standard, and its sturdy crumb survives a full ladle.

Making it

Slice a sourdough loaf half an inch thick and toast or griddle until the edges crisp and the center stays chewy. Butter is optional; the gravy usually provides the fat.

Serving it

Under tangy gravies and mushroom gravy, and beside eggs at diner breakfasts where it has largely replaced white toast.

Swaps & pairings

Griddled in olive oil it suits Sunday gravy ladled over as an open plate. Rubbed with garlic, it becomes the Italian-American toast base.
