Plates & Classics

The composed dishes: the plates gravy built, from creamed chipped beef on toast to chicken-fried steak under cream gravy. Each plate profiled with its gravy, its base, and its home ground.

Creamed Chipped Beef on Toast (SOS)

Creamed chipped beef on toast: dried beef folded into a white sauce over toast points. The military plate — SOS — that came home from the mess hall to the diner.

Plates & Classics · The Military Tradition

Sausage Gravy over Biscuits

The signature plate of the gravy world: split buttermilk biscuits under a full ladle of sausage gravy. The dish biscuits and gravy means, everywhere it is served.

Plates & Classics · The Deep South

Chicken-Fried Steak with Cream Gravy

A tenderized round steak, battered and fried like chicken, drowned in country cream or pepper gravy. The Texas plate that defines cream gravy's purpose.

Plates & Classics · Texas

Open-Faced Hot Turkey

Sliced roast turkey on bread, open-faced, covered in turkey gravy with mashed potatoes alongside. The diner special built from the holiday bird.

Plates & Classics · Midwest Diner Belt

Hot Beef Plate

Sliced roast beef on bread with mashed potatoes, the whole plate flooded with brown gravy. The Midwest diner's blue-plate standard.

Plates & Classics · Midwest Diner Belt

Country Ham with Red-Eye Gravy

A thick slice of fried country ham with its red-eye gravy, biscuits on the side for sopping. The upland South's original gravy plate.

Plates & Classics · The Deep South

Biscuits with Chocolate Gravy

Hot buttermilk biscuits split and covered in chocolate gravy, the sweet breakfast plate of the Ozarks and Appalachia. A treat plate, made for weekends and grandchildren.

Plates & Classics · The Ozarks

Tomato Gravy over Grits

A bowl of stone-ground grits under brick-red tomato gravy, the Lowcountry supper plate. Summer's answer to shrimp and grits.

Shrimp Gravy over Grits

Shrimp gravy spooned over stone-ground grits: the Lowcountry's defining plate, born a fishermen's breakfast and now its most famous export.

Hamburger Gravy over Toast

Ground-beef gravy ladled over toast points: the cafeteria and farmhouse descendant of SOS, and a main-dish plate in its own right.

Fried Chicken with Cream Gravy

Buttermilk fried chicken served with the cream gravy made from its own drippings. The Sunday-dinner plate of the South.

Pork Chop with Sawmill Gravy

A fried pork chop under sawmill gravy, the Appalachian farmhouse plate. One pan makes both: the chop first, the gravy from its drippings.

Liver and Onion Gravy Plate

Floured liver slices fried and returned to their pan gravy with caramelized onions. The diner plate that regulars order by heart.

Crawfish Gravy over Cornbread

Dark-roux crawfish gravy over a split wedge of skillet cornbread. The Louisiana breakfast plate of crawfish season.

Eggs with Gravy (Diner Style)

Fried or scrambled eggs served under sausage or milk gravy, diner style. The plate where the gravy goes on the eggs themselves.
