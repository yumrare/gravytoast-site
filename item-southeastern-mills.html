About it

Southeastern Mills, based in Rome, Georgia, built its name on gravy mixes for the Southern foodservice trade — the packets behind diner counters — before retail shelves followed. Country and sausage gravies are its flagships.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its sausage gravy mix is the foodservice form of this catalog's sausage gravy, engineered to hold on a steam table without breaking.

Serving it

Behind Southern diner and cafeteria counters first, and increasingly on retail shelves in the region it feeds.

Swaps & pairings

The catalog distinguishes foodservice-first makers from retail-first ones; Southeastern Mills is the clearest foodservice case in the eight.
