Mushroom Gravy

A gravy built on browned mushrooms and their liquor, roux-thickened with stock. Earthy and dark, it serves both as a meat gravy and as the standard vegetarian stand-in.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionPennsylvania Dutch Country
Classic baseRoast meats, mashed potatoes, egg noodles
Prep timeAbout 25 minutes
StorageRefrigerate sealed; mushrooms darken it further as it sits.
Shelf life3 to 4 days refrigerated; freezes well
Best usesRoasts, holiday plates, mashed potatoes, noodles, cutlets
SubstitutesVegetable stock makes it fully vegetarian; dried porcini deepen it
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Pennsylvania is mushroom country — Kennett Square calls itself the mushroom capital of the world — and its gravies show it. Mushroom gravy also became the default vegetarian gravy because mushrooms brown like meat in a pan.

Making it

Brown sliced mushrooms hard in fat until their liquid cooks off and they take color. Add flour, then stock, and simmer; the mushroom liquor darkens the gravy as it thickens. Thyme and garlic are the usual seasonings.

Serving it

Over roasts, meatloaf, Salisbury steak, and holiday plates where a meatless gravy is needed at the same table.

Swaps & pairings

A spoon of soy sauce or miso adds the savoriness meat stock would give. For a creamy version, finish with a splash of cream off the heat.
