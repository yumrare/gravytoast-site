Bacon Gravy

A milk gravy built purely on bacon drippings, flour whisked into the rendered fat, crumbled bacon folded back in. The breakfast-table gravy of farmhouse kitchens.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseBiscuits or toast
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes
StorageRefrigerate sealed; the fat re-melts into the gravy on reheating.
Shelf life3 days refrigerated; reheats with a whisk
Best usesBreakfast biscuits, toast, eggs, tomato plates
SubstitutesSalt-pork drippings; sausage fat for a mixed-meat pan
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Bacon gravy is the farmhouse cook's dividend: a pan of bacon was cooked most mornings, and its fat was the day's first ingredient. The gravy tastes of smoke in a way sausage gravy never quite does.

Making it

Fry bacon crisp and set it aside. Whisk flour into the drippings, then milk to a simmer, and season with pepper. Crumble the bacon back over the top at serving.

Serving it

Over biscuits or toast at breakfast, beside fried eggs. In farm country it also lands on sliced tomatoes in summer.

Swaps & pairings

Keep the bacon out of the pot and crisp on top, so it stays crisp. A spoon of maple syrup in the pan is a Northern farmhouse habit.
