Chicken Gravy

A pale, rich gravy from roast or fried chicken drippings and stock, flour-thickened. The gravy of Sunday chicken dinners, mashed potatoes, and chicken-fried chicken.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionThe Deep South
Classic baseFried or roast chicken and mashed potatoes
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes, in the chicken pan
StorageRefrigerate sealed; skim set fat before reheating if it pooled.
Shelf life3 days refrigerated; reheats with a whisk
Best usesChicken dinners, mashed potatoes, rice, chicken-fried chicken
SubstitutesTurkey gravy in the same method; stock plus cream without drippings
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chicken gravy is the Sunday gravy of the South — not the Italian-American kind, but the pan gravy of the fried or roasted chicken dinner. Cooks judge it by gloss: it should shine and coat, never paste.

Making it

Make a blond roux in the chicken drippings, then whisk in chicken stock and a splash of milk or cream. Simmer until glossy. The fond from frying carries a deeper flavor than roasting drippings.

Serving it

Over fried and roast chicken, mashed potatoes, and rice. On a chicken-fried chicken breast it is poured exactly like country gravy on steak.

Swaps & pairings

Add the chopped chicken liver from the giblets for an old-style richness. For chicken and dumplings, thin the same gravy with stock.
