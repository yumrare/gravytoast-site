Toasts & Bases

Rye Toast

Caraway-flecked rye bread, toasted. A deli and Pennsylvania Dutch base whose spice flatters mushroom, onion, and milk gravies.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionPennsylvania Dutch Country, from Europe
Classic baseMilk gravy, mushroom gravy, onion gravy
Prep time2 to 3 minutes to toast
StorageSealed at room temperature; rye keeps longer than white loaves.
Shelf lifeThe loaf keeps 5 to 6 days sealed
Best usesGravy plates, deli breakfasts, open-faced sandwiches
SubstitutesPumpernickel for depth; sourdough for tang without caraway
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Rye under gravy is an Eastern European inheritance carried into American delicatessens and Pennsylvania farm kitchens. Its caraway note makes plain milk gravy taste seasoned all by itself.

Making it

Toast rye until crisp at the edges; its dense crumb softens slowly, so it can be toasted ahead a few minutes. Light and dark rye both serve; marble rye is the deli standard.

Serving it

Under mushroom and onion gravies, beside eggs, and as the base for open-faced plates in deli service.

Swaps & pairings

Without caraway — Jewish-style rye varies — it reads plainer. Griddled in butter it carries sausage gravy like a deli breakfast.
