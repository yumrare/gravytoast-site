Toasts & Bases

English Muffin

A fork-split, griddled muffin with a pocked interior made to catch gravy. The breakfast-sandwich bread that also serves as a plated gravy base.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionNew York, from England
Classic baseSausage gravy, eggs and gravy
Prep time3 to 4 minutes to toast
StorageSealed at room temperature or frozen; refresh in the toaster.
Shelf lifeKeeps a week sealed; freezes excellently
Best usesBreakfast plates, gravy bases, egg dishes
SubstitutesA split-top roll; a biscuit for the Southern plate
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The English muffin's American story begins in New York in the 1880s, when Samuel Bath Thomas began baking them. Fork-splitting, not slicing, is the law of the house: a knife ruins the texture gravy wants to fill.

Making it

Fork-split the muffin around its equator, then toast until the ridges crisp. Butter lightly. The interior should stay rough; every ridge is a gravy shelf.

Serving it

As a plated base under sausage or milk gravy, and beneath eggs at diner breakfasts. Halves take a measured half-ladle each.

Swaps & pairings

Whole-grain muffins hold heavier gravies. As the base for creamed chipped beef it makes a Northern diner SOS.
