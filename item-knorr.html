Knorr

A German-founded global brand, dating to 1838, whose gravy granules and mixes sell worldwide. In American stores it is the European-accented shelf mate of the domestic makers.

About it

Knorr was founded in Heilbronn, Germany, in 1838 and grew into a global bouillon and sauce house. Its gravy mixes — sold in the United States as packets and granules — carry a European brown-gravy accent.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its gravy granules dissolve straight into hot water — no roux — making it the catalog's clearest contrast to the scratch method every other entry describes.

Serving it

Supermarket shelves internationally; in the United States it anchors the imported end of the gravy aisle.

Swaps & pairings

Granule gravies trade the roux's depth for certainty; the catalog profiles the method difference without scoring it.
