Plates & Classics

Sausage Gravy over Biscuits

The signature plate of the gravy world: split buttermilk biscuits under a full ladle of sausage gravy. The dish biscuits and gravy means, everywhere it is served.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionThe Deep South
Classic baseButtermilk biscuits
Prep timeAbout 30 minutes, biscuits and gravy together
StorageStore biscuits and gravy separately; assemble hot.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; components keep 3 days separately
Best usesBreakfast plates, the featured plate, diner menus
SubstitutesBacon gravy over biscuits; sawmill gravy for the camp style
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

If GravyToast had a flag plate, this is it: the dish converts more skeptics than any other in the catalog. Biscuitville and its kin built chains on it, and every Southern family's version is the correct one.

Making it

Bake tall buttermilk biscuits; while they bake, make the sausage gravy in the sausage pan. Split the biscuits, ladle gravy over until it pools, and serve before the biscuits cool.

Serving it

Two biscuit halves per plate, gravy over and between them. Eggs ride alongside; the gravy never waits.

Swaps & pairings

Half-order with one biscuit is the diner mercy portion. Crumbled biscuit under the gravy, diner-counter style, eats softer and faster.
