Bisto

About it

Bisto was created in Manchester in 1908 — its name an acronym, 'Browns, Seasons and Thickens in One' — and its granules became the default gravy of British home cooking. Its 'Aah! Bisto' slogan is a piece of British food culture.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its granules stir into hot water or meat juices off the boil — the no-roux method — producing the glossy brown gravy of the British roast dinner.

Serving it

British roast tables first; in the United States it appears in import aisles and expatriate kitchens, and beside onion gravy for sausages.

Swaps & pairings

Bisto documents the catalog's boundary from the other side: a granule gravy with no roux at all, thicker than au jus, thinner than scratch.
