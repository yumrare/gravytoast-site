About it

Morrison's, milled in Denton, Texas, is the small-miller case among the eight: a regional Texas brand whose country gravy and pepper gravy mixes ride biscuit culture the way its corn kits ride cornbread.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its peppered gravy mix is mixed to the Texas café measure — milk, five minutes, cracked pepper on top — the packet form of this catalog's pepper gravy.

Serving it

Texas and Southern supermarket shelves, and the home kitchens that keep a house brand across generations.

Swaps & pairings

Morrison's and Pioneer together show Texas gravy's two scales: the regional miller and the national brand, both born in the same state.
