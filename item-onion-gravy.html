Onion Gravy

A brown gravy built on slowly caramelized onions, sweet and deep. The classic partner of sausages, liver and onions, and smothered pork chops.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseSausages, liver, and smothered chops
Prep timeAbout 40 minutes, mostly onion time
StorageRefrigerate sealed; the onions keep sweetening it overnight.
Shelf life4 days refrigerated; freezes well
Best usesBangers and mash, liver plates, smothered meats, shepherd's pie
SubstitutesShallots for a finer version; mushroom gravy for a meatless pan
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Onion gravy is British at root — the bangers-and-mash gravy — and naturalized in American diners, where caramelized onions sweeten the smothered plates. The onions must cook slowly; a rushed onion gravy is just gravy with onions in it.

Making it

Slice onions thin and cook them in fat over low heat 25 to 30 minutes until deep gold. Stir in flour, then stock, and simmer until the onions melt into the gravy. A splash of vinegar or Worcestershire sharpens it.

Serving it

Ladled over sausages and mashed potatoes, liver and onions, and any smothered plate. It is a gravy with the vegetable built in.

Swaps & pairings

Add a spoon of mustard for the British pub style. For liver and onions, make it in the liver pan so the fond joins the onions.
