Midwest Diner Belt

The diner belt of the Midwest serves brown gravy, hot beef plates, and fried mush under gravy. Its diners and cafeterias standardized the American gravy plate.

Brown Gravy

The all-purpose gravy: a browned roux loosened with beef, chicken, or mixed stock, dark and savory. The default gravy of diners, roasts, and mashed potatoes everywhere.

Gravies · Midwest Diner Belt

Onion Gravy

A brown gravy built on slowly caramelized onions, sweet and deep. The classic partner of sausages, liver and onions, and smothered pork chops.

Gravies · Midwest Diner Belt

Liver Gravy

The pan gravy made where liver was fried: drippings, flour, and water or milk, poured back over liver and caramelized onions. A diner and farmhouse plate of the Midwest belt.

Gravies · Midwest Diner Belt

Bacon Gravy

A milk gravy built purely on bacon drippings, flour whisked into the rendered fat, crumbled bacon folded back in. The breakfast-table gravy of farmhouse kitchens.

Gravies · Midwest Diner Belt

White Toast

Plain toasted sandwich bread, the universal gravy base. Under creamed chipped beef it becomes SOS; under hamburger gravy, a cafeteria classic.

Toasts & Bases · United States diners

Sourdough Toast

Tangy, open-crumb toast from a levain loaf. Its acidity cuts rich gravies, and its crust stands up to a full ladle better than soft breads.

Toasts & Bases · United States, from European levain baking

Whole Wheat Toast

Toast from a whole-grain sandwich loaf: denser, nuttier, and sturdier than white. The lighter-feeling base that still holds a gravy line.

Toasts & Bases · United States

Hash-Brown Base

Shredded potatoes fried crisp on the griddle, used as a plated base. The breakfast-stack foundation: potatoes, eggs, and gravy in layers.

Toasts & Bases · Midwest Diner Belt

Fried Mush

Cooked cornmeal chilled firm, sliced, and fried crisp. The Midwest and Appalachian leftover base that takes gravy or syrup with equal grace.

Toasts & Bases · Midwest and Appalachia

Leftover Dressing Base

Leftover baked dressing or stuffing, reheated crisp, used as a base for turkey and giblet gravy. The day-after plate that justifies making extra.

Toasts & Bases · United States holiday kitchens

Open-Faced Hot Turkey

Sliced roast turkey on bread, open-faced, covered in turkey gravy with mashed potatoes alongside. The diner special built from the holiday bird.

Hot Beef Plate

Sliced roast beef on bread with mashed potatoes, the whole plate flooded with brown gravy. The Midwest diner's blue-plate standard.

Liver and Onion Gravy Plate

Floured liver slices fried and returned to their pan gravy with caramelized onions. The diner plate that regulars order by heart.

Eggs with Gravy (Diner Style)

Fried or scrambled eggs served under sausage or milk gravy, diner style. The plate where the gravy goes on the eggs themselves.

Smothered Steak

Round steak braised tender in brown onion gravy. The diner and farmhouse method for turning a tough cut into a supper plate.

Breakfast Stack

Hash browns layered with eggs and sausage, flooded with sausage gravy. The diner breakfast stack: every breakfast item in one plate, gravy over all.
