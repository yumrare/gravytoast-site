Eggs with Gravy (Diner Style)

Fried or scrambled eggs served under sausage or milk gravy, diner style. The plate where the gravy goes on the eggs themselves.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseToast or biscuits beneath
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes
StorageNothing stores; it is cooked to order.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate, entirely
Best usesBreakfast plates, diner menus, all-day breakfast
SubstitutesBacon gravy over eggs; tomato gravy for the garden version
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Eggs with gravy is the plate that separates gravy households from others: the gravy is not beside the eggs, it is on them. Fried eggs take it best, their yolks thinning the gravy into a second sauce.

Making it

Set toast or a split biscuit on the plate, fry eggs to order, lay them over the base, and ladle hot sausage or milk gravy across the eggs. Serve immediately.

Serving it

Eggs center-plate under the gravy, hash browns eating what runs off. A full breakfast in one plate, no sides required.

Swaps & pairings

Scrambled eggs take the gravy folded through instead. A fried egg's broken yolk in the gravy is the counter cook's finish.
