Breakfast Stack

Hash browns layered with eggs and sausage, flooded with sausage gravy. The diner breakfast stack: every breakfast item in one plate, gravy over all.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionMidwest Diner Belt
Classic baseHash browns

Best usesBreakfast plates, diner menus, all-day breakfast

Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

The breakfast stack is the diner line's architecture: potato platform, egg layer, gravy roof. It exists because a short-order cook plates what the pass gives him — and because it eats even better than it stacks.

Making it

Crisp a hash-brown platform on the griddle; top with a sausage patty and fried or scrambled eggs; ladle sausage gravy over the whole stack and serve before the crisp goes.

Serving it

Stacked, not spread: the plate arrives tall and settles as it eats. Cheese between the layers is the modern diner addition.

Swaps & pairings

On a split biscuit it leaves the griddle for the sandwich board. With bacon gravy and a fried egg it is the belt's other stack.
