Vegan Cashew Gravy

A dairy-free white gravy of blended soaked cashews, water, and nutritional yeast, seasoned with black pepper and sage. Creamy without milk or butter; the modern plant-based stand-in for sausage gravy.

CategoryGravies
Origin / RegionModern United States
Classic baseBiscuits
Prep timeAbout 15 minutes, plus cashew soaking
StorageRefrigerate sealed; thin with water when reheating.
Shelf life3 to 4 days refrigerated; thickens as it chills
Best usesBiscuits, roasted vegetables, potatoes, vegan plates
SubstitutesBlended white beans or oat cream for a nut-free version
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Cashew gravy comes from the plant-based kitchens that reverse-engineered sausage gravy: the body from nuts, the savor from yeast and sage. It thickens as it cools, so it is served a shade thinner than it looks.

Making it

Blend soaked raw cashews with water until completely smooth. Simmer with nutritional yeast, salt, black pepper, and sage until it thickens to a pourable cream. A mushroom base can replace part of the cashews.

Serving it

Over biscuits for a plant-based biscuits-and-gravy, and over roasted vegetables and potatoes anywhere milk gravy would go.

Swaps & pairings

Add crumbled plant-based sausage and it mirrors sausage gravy. Smoked paprika gives it the pan-dripping note it otherwise lacks.
