Toasts & Bases

What gravy goes on: toast in every cut, biscuits, muffins, cornbread, grits, and the starches that carry a plate. Every base profiled with its texture, its classic gravy, and its swaps.

White Toast

Plain toasted sandwich bread, the universal gravy base. Under creamed chipped beef it becomes SOS; under hamburger gravy, a cafeteria classic.

Toasts & Bases · United States diners

Texas Toast

White bread cut twice as thick as standard, about an inch, griddled in butter. The Texas steakhouse and diner base for pepper gravy and chicken-fried plates.

Toasts & Bases · Texas

Sourdough Toast

Tangy, open-crumb toast from a levain loaf. Its acidity cuts rich gravies, and its crust stands up to a full ladle better than soft breads.

Toasts & Bases · United States, from European levain baking

Whole Wheat Toast

Toast from a whole-grain sandwich loaf: denser, nuttier, and sturdier than white. The lighter-feeling base that still holds a gravy line.

Toasts & Bases · United States

Rye Toast

Caraway-flecked rye bread, toasted. A deli and Pennsylvania Dutch base whose spice flatters mushroom, onion, and milk gravies.

Toasts & Bases · Pennsylvania Dutch Country, from Europe

English Muffin

A fork-split, griddled muffin with a pocked interior made to catch gravy. The breakfast-sandwich bread that also serves as a plated gravy base.

Toasts & Bases · New York, from England

Buttermilk Biscuit

The Southern buttermilk biscuit: tall, tender, and tangy, built to be split and gravied. The single most important base in this catalog.

Toasts & Bases · The Deep South

Split-Top Roll

A soft white roll with a split baked into its top, the New England bakery roll. Toasted in butter, it is the base of hot sandwiches and gravy plates.

Toasts & Bases · New England

Cornbread

A wedge or square of skillet cornbread, split or crumbled as the base. In Louisiana it carries crawfish gravy; in the upland South, cornmeal and tomato gravies.

Toasts & Bases · The Deep South

Grits

Ground hominy corn cooked to a soft porridge, the Lowcountry's base. Under shrimp gravy it is the region's defining plate; under tomato gravy, its supper form.

Toasts & Bases · The Lowcountry

Hash-Brown Base

Shredded potatoes fried crisp on the griddle, used as a plated base. The breakfast-stack foundation: potatoes, eggs, and gravy in layers.

Toasts & Bases · Midwest Diner Belt

Fried Mush

Cooked cornmeal chilled firm, sliced, and fried crisp. The Midwest and Appalachian leftover base that takes gravy or syrup with equal grace.

Toasts & Bases · Midwest and Appalachia

Johnnycake

A cornmeal griddle cake of Rhode Island tradition, thin and crisp-edged. A New England base that takes milk gravy, syrup, or gravy-and-ham plates.

Toasts & Bases · New England — Rhode Island

Bagel

A boiled-then-baked ring roll with a chewy crust, split and toasted. A New York base that shrugs off heavy gravies and anchors deli breakfast plates.

Toasts & Bases · New York City, via Poland

Leftover Dressing Base

Leftover baked dressing or stuffing, reheated crisp, used as a base for turkey and giblet gravy. The day-after plate that justifies making extra.

Toasts & Bases · United States holiday kitchens
