Plates & Classics

Chicken-Fried Chicken

A pounded chicken breast, battered and fried like chicken-fried steak, under cream or pepper gravy. The Texas method applied to poultry.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionTexas
Classic baseThe breast itself, with mashed potatoes
Prep timeAbout 30 minutes
StorageStore chicken and gravy separately; reheat the chicken in an oven to keep the crust.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; chicken keeps 3 days
Best usesSupper plates, Texas cafés, steakhouse lunches
SubstitutesChicken-fried steak, the original of the method
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Chicken-fried chicken exists because the CFS method was too good to leave to beef. Pounded flat, the breast fries fast and stays juicy; pepper gravy makes it the steakhouse lunch it has become.

Making it

Pound a chicken breast even, dredge in seasoned flour, egg wash, flour again, and fry until deeply golden. Make pepper or cream gravy in the pan drippings and pour over.

Serving it

Breast center-plate under a full pour of gravy, mashed potatoes catching the rest. The chicken-fried steak's equal at Texas cafés.

Swaps & pairings

Pepper gravy for the steakhouse plate, plain cream gravy for the café. A boneless thigh fries juicier and is the cook's choice.
