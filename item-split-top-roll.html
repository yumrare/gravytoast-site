Toasts & Bases

Split-Top Roll

A soft white roll with a split baked into its top, the New England bakery roll. Toasted in butter, it is the base of hot sandwiches and gravy plates.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionNew England
Classic baseTurkey gravy, chicken gravy
Prep timeAbout 4 minutes on the griddle
StorageSealed at room temperature; day-old rolls griddle better than fresh.
Shelf life2 to 3 days sealed; refresh on the griddle
Best usesHot sandwiches, gravy plates, diner specials
SubstitutesA hoagie roll; thick white toast
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Split-top rolls were engineered by New England bakeries in the 1940s for lobster rolls, but diners adopted them for everything soft bread does. The split takes gravy down the middle like a trough.

Making it

Butter the flat sides and griddle until golden; the split opens as it warms. Serve at once, split-side up, ready to be filled and gravied.

Serving it

Under open-faced hot turkey and chicken plates, where the split catches the gravy, and as the classic lobster roll shell off-season from this catalog.

Swaps & pairings

Un-toasted and cold it leaves this catalog for the seafood counter. Toasted dark, it carries hamburger gravy like a bun.
