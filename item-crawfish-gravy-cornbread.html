Crawfish Gravy over Cornbread

Dark-roux crawfish gravy over a split wedge of skillet cornbread. The Louisiana breakfast plate of crawfish season.

CategoryPlates & Classics
Origin / RegionNew Orleans
Classic baseCornbread
Prep timeAbout 45 minutes
StorageStore gravy sealed; cornbread at room temperature.
Shelf lifeA serve-at-once plate; gravy keeps 2 to 3 days
Best usesBreakfast plates, crawfish season, brunch
SubstitutesShrimp gravy over cornbread, out of crawfish season
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Crawfish over cornbread is the Louisiana breakfast the rest of the state eats while visitors order beignets. The cornbread must be unsweetened and skillet-crisped — the gravy brings plenty of richness on its own.

Making it

Split a wedge of day-old cornbread and toast its cut side in butter. Make the crawfish gravy on a dark roux with the trinity, and ladle it over the cornbread until the wedge drinks its fill.

Serving it

Cornbread on the plate, gravy over, green onions scattered. Coffee alongside; it is breakfast, whatever the clock says.

Swaps & pairings

Over rice it is the supper form. A fried egg on top is the New Orleans brunch reading.
