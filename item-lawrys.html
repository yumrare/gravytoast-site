About it

Lawry's began with a Los Angeles restaurant — Lawry's The Prime Rib, opened 1938 — and the seasoned salt its tables made famous. Its gravy mixes carry the brand's steakhouse accent into the packet aisle.

Making it

Profiled, not cooked: its brown gravy mix is seasoned in the house style, closer to a steakhouse pan sauce than a plain diner brown — the packet kin of this catalog's au jus boundary case.

Serving it

National supermarket shelves, and steakhouse-adjacent home plates: roasts, prime rib nights, and holiday beef.

Swaps & pairings

Lawry's shows how a restaurant brand becomes a grocery brand; the catalog profiles it as the steakhouse case among the eight.
