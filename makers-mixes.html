Makers & Mixes

The makers of gravy mixes and the brands behind the diner counter: national mix makers profiled neutrally, like every entry, with what they make and where they come from.

Pioneer Brand

A Texas flour-and-mix brand, founded in San Antonio in 1851, whose gravy mixes are the packet standard for country and pepper gravy across the South.

Makers & Mixes · San Antonio, Texas

McCormick

The American spice house, founded 1889, whose gravy mixes and jars are the national supermarket standard for brown, turkey, and country gravies.

Makers & Mixes · Hunt Valley, Maryland

Southeastern Mills

A Rome, Georgia maker of gravy mixes, built on the Southern foodservice trade. Its country and sausage gravy mixes are the packets behind many diner counters.

Makers & Mixes · Rome, Georgia

Knorr

A German-founded global brand, dating to 1838, whose gravy granules and mixes sell worldwide. In American stores it is the European-accented shelf mate of the domestic makers.

Makers & Mixes · Heilbronn, Germany

Lawry's

A Los Angeles-born brand, founded on a 1938 steakhouse and its seasoned salt, whose gravy mixes bring a steakhouse accent to the packet aisle.

Makers & Mixes · Los Angeles, California

Bisto

The British gravy institution, created in 1908, whose granules are the default gravy of United Kingdom home cooking. Sold in the United States as an import specialty.

Makers & Mixes · Manchester, England

Durkee

An American spice house dating to the 1850s, whose gravy mixes are among the older packet traditions on the shelf: plain, pepper-forward diner gravies.

Makers & Mixes · United States, founded in New York

Morrison's

A Denton, Texas milling brand whose country and pepper gravy mixes are a regional staple — the small-miller counterpart to the national packet makers.

Makers & Mixes · Denton, Texas
