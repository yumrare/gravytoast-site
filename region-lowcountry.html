The Lowcountry

The Carolina and Georgia Lowcountry pairs its gravies with stone-ground grits: shrimp gravy at breakfast, tomato gravy over grits at supper.

Shrimp Gravy

A Lowcountry gravy of shrimp, bacon or ham, peppers, and a light roux, made to pour over stone-ground grits. A breakfast and supper dish of the Carolina and Georgia coast.

Grits

Ground hominy corn cooked to a soft porridge, the Lowcountry's base. Under shrimp gravy it is the region's defining plate; under tomato gravy, its supper form.

Tomato Gravy over Grits

A bowl of stone-ground grits under brick-red tomato gravy, the Lowcountry supper plate. Summer's answer to shrimp and grits.
