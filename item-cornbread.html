Toasts & Bases

Cornbread

A wedge or square of skillet cornbread, split or crumbled as the base. In Louisiana it carries crawfish gravy; in the upland South, cornmeal and tomato gravies.

CategoryToasts & Bases
Origin / RegionThe Deep South
Classic baseCrawfish gravy, tomato gravy, cornmeal gravy
Prep timeAbout 30 minutes to bake
StorageWrap and keep at room temperature; dry cornbread is a feature here.
Shelf life2 days sealed; stale cornbread is the better base
Best usesGravy bases, crawfish plates, soup and greens plates
SubstitutesJohnnycake in New England style; fried mush for a crisp base
Last checkedOctober 18, 2026

About it

Cornbread's role under gravy is old camp and farm logic: yesterday's cornbread is today's base. Its crumb drinks gravy faster than toast, so plates are served and eaten promptly.

Making it

Bake cornbread in a preheated cast-iron skillet for a crisp crust. For gravy service, split a wedge, toast the cut side in butter if it is a day old, and plate crust-down.

Serving it

Split under crawfish gravy in Louisiana, crumbled in a bowl under tomato gravy in the uplands, and beside every bean and greens plate.

Swaps & pairings

Sweet Northern cornbread and unsweetened Southern skillet bread divide families; both carry gravy, and the catalog takes no side.
